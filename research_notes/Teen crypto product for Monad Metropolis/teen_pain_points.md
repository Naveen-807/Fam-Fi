# Teen (13–19) and Parent Money Pain Points, 2024–2026

Scope note: about 16 web searches and fetches. Most findings come from primary sources (Stripe and Google help pages, Western Union FAQ, Piper Sandler, RBI coverage, Comptroller reports) or company surveys, which are labelled as such. **No verbatim Reddit or app-store quotes were collected** (see Gaps). Anything marked "Inference" is my analysis, not a sourced finding.

---

## 1. What do teens complain about (allowances, P2P, online purchases, gaming, subscriptions, saving, independence, scams)?

### Takeaway
Teens' most visible money pain is **being under 18**. They cannot hold their own cards, payout accounts or remittance-receiving accounts, so almost every digital money action goes through a parent's account. The best-quantified social pain is group cost-sharing: being fronted money, not getting paid back, and arguments with friends. Teen spending is flat to falling, and teens feel economic pessimism.

### Cited Findings
- **Spending levels (US):**
  - Spring 2025: self-reported annual teen spending was about $2,388, up roughly 6% YoY. The sample was 6,455 teens. — [Piper Sandler / BusinessWire, Apr 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250409407391/en/Piper-Sandler-Completes-49th-Semi-Annual-Taking-Stock-With-Teens%C2%AE-Survey)
  - Fall 2025: spending fell to $2,213, down 6% YoY. The sample was about 10,959 teens, average age 15.7. — [Piper Sandler 50th survey](https://www.pipersandler.com/news/piper-sandler-completes-50th-semi-annual-teen-survey); [SGB Online](https://sgbonline.com/piper-sandlers-taking-stock-with-teens-becomes-an-annual-event/)
  - Stylus reports the multi-year series as $2,419 (2023), $2,263 (2024) and $2,213 (2025). The 2024 figure conflicts with the spring-2025 release, which describes 2025 as higher than 2024.
  - 62% of US teens believe the economy is getting worse. — [Stylus, 2025](https://stylus.com/consumer-attitudes/piper-sandler-teen-survey-2025-teens-economic-uncertainty)
- **Social apps (Fall 2025, Piper Sandler):** TikTok 46% most-used, Instagram 31%, Snapchat 14%. — [Piper Sandler](https://www.pipersandler.com/news/piper-sandler-completes-50th-semi-annual-teen-survey)
- **Splitting with friends:**
  - 73% of Gen Z aged 16–26 have had at least one major negative experience from cost-sharing with friends (Cash App survey, n=1,000, April 2024). — [Cash App Gen Z Money Report](https://cash.app/press/cash-app-gen-z-money-report)
  - A Zelle-linked report found that 76% of Gen Z who fronted a group expense were not repaid, 25% said repayment issues damaged a relationship, and 14% ended a friendship over it. — [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/gen-z-spending-repayment-habits-group-events-breaks-with-older-generations-)
  - Gen Z typically has one friend front the bill and collect later. — [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/gen-z-spending-repayment-habits-group-events-breaks-with-older-generations-); see also [YPulse, Jul 2024](https://www.ypulse.com/newsfeed/2024/07/24/gen-z-is-regretting-overspending-habits-and-splitting-expenses-with-friends/)
  - Caveat: both surveys come from payment companies.
- **Online purchases without a card:**
  - Under-18s generally cannot get their own credit card. The only route is authorized-user status on a parent's card.
  - Prepaid cards, gift cards and PayPal often require 18+ or parental involvement. — [GoHenry US blog](https://www.gohenry.com/us/blog/financial-education/can-teens-get-a-credit-card-at-16-what-other-options-are-there)
  - GoHenry is an interested party.
- **P2P supervised products exist, but all are parent-owned:**
  - Cash App opened to ages 13+ via parent "Sponsored Accounts" in Nov 2021. — [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/the-ascent/banks/articles/venmo-introduces-new-teen-account-news-brief/)
  - Venmo Teen Account launched May/June 2023 for ages 13–17. Only a parent can open it, a parent can oversee up to 5 teens, and the parent can see balances, transactions and friend lists and can block users. — [PYMNTS](https://pymnts.com/?p=1508474); [PhoneArena](https://www.phonearena.com/news/parents-can-now-set-up-and-manage-venmo-accounts-for-their-teens_id147666)
- **Scams (youth lose money more often):**
  - FTC 2024 data shows younger people report losing money more often than older people. 44% of people in their 20s who reported fraud lost money, vs 24% of people in their 70s. The median loss for people in their 20s was $417 (via secondary reporting). — [Moneywise/AOL](https://www.aol.com/finance/2-groups-now-most-risk-191500987.html); [FKKS summary of FTC top scams 2024](https://advertisinglaw.fkks.com/post/102k3c2/ftc-announces-top-scams-of-2024)
  - FBI 2022: 15,782 scam reports from people aged 19 and under, with losses of $210.5M. Social Catfish (a vendor) claims losses among people aged 20 and under grew about 2,500% over 5 years. — [WBTV/InvestigateTV, Jan 2024](https://www.wbtv.com/2024/01/23/scams-targeting-teens-have-skyrocketed-recent-years-study-finds)
  - FBI warns of financial sextortion targeting minors. Victims are typically males aged 14–17, and offenders are usually outside the US. — [CalCoastNews / FBI](https://calcoastnews.com/2024/01/fbi-warns-about-sextortion-schemes-targeting-minors/)
  - Scam types aimed at teens include fake influencer or brand contests that demand a fee or bank details, and gaming-item link scams. — [WBTV](https://www.wbtv.com/2024/01/23/scams-targeting-teens-have-skyrocketed-recent-years-study-finds)

### Inferences
- The recurring teen complaint is dependency rather than lack of money. Every rail (card, P2P, payout, remittance) puts a parent's identity in the loop.
- Splitting and fronting money is the clearest peer-to-peer problem that has numbers attached. It is a natural wedge for a social, programmable product, for example escrowed group pools or auto-settle.

### Gaps
- No verbatim Reddit (r/teenagers, r/personalfinance) or app-store quotes were collected.
- No 2024–26 survey quantifies teen complaints specifically about subscriptions, game top-ups being blocked, or the lack of independence.
- Piper Sandler's payment-method data (e.g., Apple Pay or Cash App share) is not in the public releases.

## 2. What do parents worry about and pay for (Greenlight/GoHenry as proxy)?

### Takeaway
Parents pay **$4–$20/month** for visibility, spending controls, safety features (location, crash detection, identity protection) and financial-literacy content. The higher tiers bundle **safety** rather than money features. Worry about in-game spending is real, but most of the data is older or European.

### Cited Findings
- **Greenlight (2026 pricing, per family):**
  - Core $5.99/mo.
  - Max $10.98/mo: investing, 1% cashback, 3% on savings, protection plans.
  - Infinity $15.98/mo: adds location sharing and crash detection.
  - Family Shield $19.98/mo (one deals site cites a $24.98 regular price).
  - Safe Family device add-on $4.99/mo.
  - One Greenlight page shows Infinity at $14.98, which looks stale.
  - Sources: [Greenlight Help](https://help.greenlight.com/hc/en-us/articles/235739508-How-much-does-Greenlight-cost); [Greenlight plans](https://greenlight.com/which-greenlight-plan-should-i-choose)
- **GoHenry UK:**
  - Everyday £3.99/mo per child: 1 free top-up, then 50p each.
  - Plus £5.99/mo per child: unlimited top-ups, 1% cashback, savings interest.
  - Max £9.99/mo for up to 4 children.
  - Custom card £4.99.
  - The US base price was reported at $3.99/child (undated review).
  - Sources: [GoHenry UK pricing](https://gohenry.com/uk/pricing); [Finder UK](https://www.finder.com/uk/gohenry-review); [KidsMoney](https://www.kidsmoney.org/parents/money-management/gohenry-review)
- **In-game spending:**
  - 2024 Ipsos survey across 5 European markets: 95% of parents of children who buy in-game extras actively monitor that spending. 76% of parents of child gamers say their kids make no in-game purchases. Among spenders, the average fell from €39 to €31 (−21% vs 2023). — [Video Games Europe 2024](https://www.videogameseurope.eu/publication/in-game-purchases-in-european-markets-2024/)
  - 2020 (older) US survey: 67.1% of parents worry their kids overspend on mobile games, and 40.8% say their child made in-app purchases without their knowledge. — [SellCell](https://www.sellcell.com/blog/more-than-two-thirds-of-parents-worry-kids-overspending-on-in-app-purchases)
  - In Aug 2024, 42% of Roblox's roughly 380M users were under 13 (cited in a 2025 academic presentation). — [DiGRA](https://dl.digra.org/index.php/dl/article/download/2587/2580)
- **Venmo Teen's parent controls** show what parents value: visibility of balances, transactions and friend lists, plus the ability to block contacts. — [PhoneArena](https://www.phonearena.com/news/parents-can-now-set-up-and-manage-venmo-accounts-for-their-teens_id147666)

### Inferences
- Greenlight's tier ladder suggests parents' willingness to pay rises with **safety and peace of mind** (location, crash detection, identity protection) more than with money features. A programmable-money product would need to sell control and safety (allowlists, spend rules, scam blocking), not yield.
- Venmo and Cash App teen accounts are free, so a basic supervised P2P product has been commoditized in the US.

### Gaps
- No 2024–26 US survey on parent worry about Roblox or Fortnite spending specifically.
- No Greenlight or GoHenry churn or ARPU disclosures.
- No direct survey ranking parent worries (overspending vs scams vs literacy).

## 3. Teen online earning: how do under-18 creators and freelancers get paid?

### Takeaway
Nearly every mainstream payout rail requires an **adult account holder**: AdSense for YouTube, Stripe Express/Custom Connect, Western Union, the PayPal-linked Tipalti step for Roblox DevEx, and Upwork/Fiverr in most cases. Teens either route earnings through a parent or cannot get paid at all. The rules are verified. How many teens this affects is **not quantified** in any source found.

### Cited Findings
- **YouTube:** the minimum age for AdSense for YouTube is 18. A minor can only monetize by linking the channel to a parent or guardian's approved AdSense account, and payments go to the adult. — [Google AdSense Help](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/2533300?hl=en); [AdSense eligibility](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9724?hl=en)
- **Stripe:**
  - Standard accounts can be opened at 13+, but a legal guardian must take over ownership before charges or payouts if the user is under 18.
  - Express and Custom Connect accounts have a hard 18+ minimum (an under-18 birthdate gives an error). Most marketplaces use these account types.
  - Source: [Stripe Support](https://support.stripe.com/questions/age-requirement-to-create-a-stripe-account)
- **Roblox DevEx:**
  - Roblox's stated minimum is 13, with parental consent if under 18.
  - Users on the Roblox forum report that the Tipalti payout portal demands an 18+ birthdate, so a parent must enter their details. — [Roblox DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/devex-incongruent-age-requeriments/2302836)
  - A 2026 CPA guide says under-18s need a parent or guardian to set up Tipalti, and gives the threshold as 30,000 earned Robux. Older sources say 100,000, so the threshold is conflicting. — [Monaco CPA](https://www.monacocpa.cpa/post/roblox-developer-taxes-devex-guide)
- **Upwork, Fiverr and Freelancer.com:**
  - Secondary sources say Upwork generally requires 18+.
  - Sources conflict on Fiverr: one says 13–17 with a parent's account, another says legal age to contract.
  - Freelancer.com's minimum is 16.
  - Sources: [WebsiteBuilderInsider](https://www.websitebuilderinsider.com/can-you-work-under-18-at-upwork/); [NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/article/finance/make-money-online-teenager)
  - None of these are official ToS. Verify before relying on them.
- **PayPal:** secondary sources say 18+, with a parent as primary account holder. I could not retrieve PayPal's user agreement in this session. — [NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/article/finance/make-money-online-teenager)
- **Workarounds seen in the wild:** platforms built for teen workers route money to a parent. Bambino's sitter-payment help page describes a special under-18 setup. — [Bambino support](https://support.bambinoapp.com/support/solutions/articles/14000158606-how-do-payments-work-for-sitters-under-18-)

### Inferences
- A parent-co-signed, teen-held wallet that can receive from platforms (with the parent as KYC'd guardian) is a structurally plausible gap. However, platforms would still need to integrate it, and their 18+ rules also come from contract-law, tax and KYC obligations that stablecoins do not remove.
- Discord commissions and other informal payments (art, Roblox scripting, Fortnite map work) likely run on PayPal friends-and-family via parents' accounts or on gift cards. This is unverified.

### Gaps
- No data on the number of under-18 creators or their earnings, or on the share blocked by payout rules.
- No official Fiverr, Upwork or PayPal ToS text was retrieved.
- No TikTok Creator Rewards age rule was retrieved (believed to be 18+, unverified).

## 4. Group money (clubs, teams, trips, ticket splits)

### Takeaway
School booster clubs and teams commonly collect money through **personal Venmo accounts and cash**. Under a single treasurer, this repeatedly leads to embezzlement that goes undetected for years. Investigators cite missing receipts, undeposited cash and app proceeds, and falsified reports.

### Cited Findings
- **DeKalb County (TN) band boosters:**
  - At least $48,650 was misappropriated from 2021 to 2025.
  - $44,821.92 was moved from the club's Venmo account to the treasurer's or a family member's account.
  - $2,543.50 of Venmo-collected fundraising never reached the bank.
  - About $74K of disbursements lacked receipts.
  - Sources: [TN Comptroller report, 2026](https://www.comptroller.tn.gov/content/dam/cot/ia/advanced-search/2026/school-support-organization/DeKalbCountyBandBoostersReport.pdf); [NewsChannel5](https://www.newschannel5.com/news/state/tennessee/de-kalb-county/tenn-high-school-marching-band-boosters-treasurer-indicted-in-48k-theft)
- **Aliso Niguel HS (CA) football boosters:** $411,761 was allegedly diverted over about 2 years, and false financial reports were emailed to the board. — [FOX LA](https://www.foxla.com/news/aliso-niguel-high-school-booster-club-treasurer-wire-fraud-arrest)
- **Topsail High (NC):** $190,749.51 is unaccounted for, and the treasurer admitted wrongdoing (2026). — [Port City Daily](https://portcitydaily.com/latest-news/2026/04/14/topsail-high-booster-investigation-reveals-treasurer-admitted-to-wrongdoing-extent-of-questionable-activity/)
- **Liberty High (SC) boosters:** 3 people were arrested in Sept 2025 after a district review. — [FOX Carolina](https://www.foxcarolina.com/2025/09/17/deputies-3-arrested-after-money-embezzled-liberty-high-school-booster-club)
- **Wellston (OH):** a former booster treasurer was sentenced to 24 months over more than $40K. — [Ohio AG, Oct 2025](https://ohioattorneygeneral.gov/Media/News-Releases/October-2025/Former-Treasurer-Sentenced-for-Stealing-$40-000-Fr)
- **White House HS (TN) football (Sept 2026):** the treasurer allegedly moved funds to a personal Venmo account. — [WSMV](https://www.wsmv.com/2026/09/24/former-midstate-high-school-booster-club-treasurer-indicted-misappropriating-thousands-comptroller-says/)
- **Recommended controls** include separation of duties, deposit of all receipts, dual signatures and independent review of bank statements. — [TN Comptroller Model Financial Policy (via report)](https://www.comptroller.tn.gov/content/dam/cot/ia/advanced-search/2026/school-support-organization/DeKalbCountyBandBoostersReport.pdf)

### Inferences
- This is one of the **strongest cases where programmable money is materially better**:
  - A transparent shared treasury that any parent or member can audit.
  - Multisig or dual-approval spends.
  - Purpose-locked funds and automatic receipts.
- These map directly onto the controls auditors recommend and onto the failure modes in the cases above.
- Caveat: these are adult-run booster clubs. Student-run clubs and teams likely have the same problems at smaller scale (unverified).

### Gaps
- No survey quantifies how student clubs, teams or trip groups collect money, or how often it goes wrong.
- No aggregate count of booster-club fraud cases.
- No data on ticket splitting among teens specifically.

## 5. India specifics: UPI for minors, FamPay/Junio, UPI Circle

### Takeaway
India is building **parent-delegated UPI** for teens. UPI Circle lets a parent authorize a secondary user with limits or per-payment approval. Paytm "Pocket Money" (May 2026) and Junio's PPI wallet build on it, and FamApp (ex-FamPay) offers UPI and cards for ages 11+. RBI (Apr 2025) lets minors aged 10+ operate savings and term-deposit accounts independently, within bank-set limits.

### Cited Findings
- **UPI Circle mechanics:**
  - A primary holder delegates to a secondary user (child, spouse, parent and so on) without sharing the PIN.
  - Two approval modes: manual (the parent approves each payment) or automatic within a limit.
  - Parents can set limits, track in real time, revoke access, and approve or reject payments.
  - The UPI Steering Committee approved it on Aug 3, 2023.
  - Sources: [Business Today, Jun 2026](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/personal-finance/banking/story/what-is-upi-circle-how-the-new-feature-is-making-digital-payments-safer-for-families-teenagers-534941-2026-06-05); [Business Today, Aug 2024](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/personal-finance/news/story/upi-circle-what-is-the-new-feature-and-how-will-it-work-for-multiple-users-check-top-5-points-441849-2024-08-17)
- **Paytm Pocket Money (May 2026):**
  - Teens can use UPI without their own bank account.
  - Limits are Rs 5,000 per transaction and Rs 15,000 per month, with early caps for new users (Rs 500 in the first 30 minutes, Rs 5,000 within 24 hours).
  - No international payments and no cash withdrawal.
  - Sources: [The Paypers](https://thepaypers.com/payments/news/paytm-launches-pocket-money-upi-feature-for-teenagers); [APAC News Network](https://apacnewsnetwork.com/2026/05/paytm-lets-teenagers-make-upi-payments-without-bank-account-through-new-pocket-money-feature/)
- **Junio:** its subsidiary received RBI in-principle authorization for PPIs and planned a UPI-linked wallet aligned with UPI Circle. I did not confirm whether it has launched. — [MediaInfoline](https://www.mediainfoline.com/uncategorized/junio-payments-receives-rbis-in-principle-authorisation-to-issue-prepaid-payment-instruments)
- **FamApp (ex-FamPay):**
  - Offers UPI and card payments to "everyone above 11" and claims "10 million+ users" (company recruiting page, undated). — [startup.jobs / FamPay](https://startup.jobs/ux-copywriter-fampayindia-8011395)
  - Earlier: a numberless prepaid card via IDFC First Bank that needs parental consent. — [Finextra](https://www.finextra.com/newsarticle/38272/indias-fampay-raises-38m-for-teen-focused-app)
  - Third-party "20M users" claims are unreliable.
- **RBI, Apr 21 2025:**
  - Minors aged 10+ may open and operate savings and term-deposit accounts independently, up to bank-set limits.
  - Banks decide on net banking, debit cards and cheque books.
  - Sources: [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/finance/news/rbi-permits-minors-above-10-yrs-to-operate-bank-accounts-independently-125042101090_1.html); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/personal-finance/banking/story/rbi-eases-bank-account-rules-for-minors-above-the-age-of-10-what-parents-need-to-know-before-july-1-473026-2025-04-22)
- Other Indian teen fintechs named in coverage: Walrus, Akudo. — [Business Today 2026](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/personal-finance/banking/story/what-is-upi-circle-how-the-new-feature-is-making-digital-payments-safer-for-families-teenagers-534941-2026-06-05)

### Inferences
- In India, everyday domestic teen payments are **well served** by UPI Circle, Pocket Money and FamApp, at zero cost and with instant settlement. A crypto or stablecoin product will **not** beat UPI for domestic payments.
- Openings left in India include:
  - International receipts and purchases (Paytm Pocket Money bars international payments).
  - Earning from foreign platforms.
  - Group treasuries.
- Indian crypto regulation and tax (30% tax, 1% TDS) is a major headwind. That is from my background knowledge and was not verified in this session.

### Gaps
- No Indian survey or Reddit (r/IndiaInvestments) teen pain-point data was collected.
- No NPCI user numbers for UPI Circle.
- No official NPCI age rule for UPI Circle secondary users (one community post claims ages 10–18, unverified).

## 6. Teens receiving gifts or remittances from relatives abroad

### Takeaway
Major remittance providers require the **recipient to be 18+**, so money for a teen has to go to a parent. I found no data on how often this blocks teens.

### Cited Findings
- Western Union: "you must be at least 18 years old to send or receive money," and receivers must meet the age rules of the receiver's country. — [Western Union FAQ](https://www.westernunion.com/us/en/frequently-asked-questions/faq-receive-money.html); [WU Terms](https://www.westernunion.com/ht/en/terms-conditions.html)
- Paytm Pocket Money (India) blocks international payments for teen users. — [The Paypers](https://thepaypers.com/payments/news/paytm-launches-pocket-money-upi-feature-for-teenagers)

### Inferences
- Cross-border gifts to minors (Diaspora to India, Philippines, Mexico, Nigeria) are a plausible area where **stablecoins are materially better**: borderless, fast and cheap. They would still need a guardian-controlled wallet and a local off-ramp, which runs back into the age and KYC constraints.

### Gaps
- No Wise, Remitly or PayPal policy on minor recipients was retrieved.
- No survey of teens receiving cross-border gifts.
- No World Bank remittance-cost figure was gathered in this session.

## 7. Gen Z/Gen Alpha attitudes toward crypto and stablecoins; crypto scams aimed at teens

### Takeaway
Young **adults** report high crypto exposure and openness to stablecoins. Teen-specific data is thin: one UK survey found that 27% of 15–18s had used a crypto-related product but only 23% felt they understood it. Teens say they want scam and safety education.

### Cited Findings
- **UK teens:** 27% of 2,000 UK 15–18-year-olds have owned or used a crypto-related product (exchanges, wallets, NFTs, gaming tokens). 23% feel confident they understand crypto, and 52% want crypto and NFT content in financial education, with safety and scam awareness at the top. Focus groups were sceptical of finfluencer hype and alert to scams. — [BCB Group](https://www.bcbgroup.com/insights/young-people-and-cryptocurrency-what-the-next-generation-really-thinks-about-digital-finance)
  - Caveat: BCB is a crypto firm and the survey date is unclear (2025 probable).
- **Gen Z adults:** 51% of US Gen Z respondents own or have owned crypto, vs 49% of Millennials and 29% of Gen X (Gemini; fieldwork May–Jul 2024, published Jan 2025; n=6,000 across 6 countries; Gemini is an exchange). — [Gemini](https://www.gemini.com/blog/gemini-survey-finds-more-than-half-of-gen-z-owns-crypto)
- **Stablecoins:** 71% of Gen Z say they would consider using stablecoins for everyday purchases, vs about 50% overall (Motley Fool 2025). This measures intent, not use. — [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/the-ascent/research/study-americans-cryptocurrency)
- **Gen Z investors:** 42% own crypto vs 11% who hold a retirement account (YouGov via NGPF, Aug 2025). — [NGPF](https://dev8.ngpf.org/blog/question-of-the-day/question-of-the-day-what-percent-of-18-29-year-olds-have-invested-in-traded-or-used-a-cryptocurrency-eg-bitcoin-or-ether/)
- **US adults overall:** 10% used or owned crypto at end-2025, up from 7% at end-2024 (Federal Reserve SHED via Yahoo). — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/crypto/articles/survey-finds-10-u-adults-194800815.html)
- **Scams:** investment scams were a top loss category in the FTC's 2024 data, and social-media contact was linked to losses. — [FKKS/FTC](https://advertisinglaw.fkks.com/post/102k3c2/ftc-announces-top-scams-of-2024)

### Inferences
- A **crypto-naive majority** of teens plus a **crypto-curious minority** (~1 in 4 in the UK) suggests a product should hide the chain, framing it as a "dollar wallet" or "group pot", rather than lead with crypto.
- Teens' own priority of scam and safety education lines up with what parents pay for.

### Gaps
- No US teen (13–17) crypto ownership survey from 2024–26 was found. Piper Sandler's crypto questions, if any, are not public.
- No data on crypto-specific scams targeting minors (e.g., Discord or Roblox "free Robux" wallet drainers).
- No Morgan Stanley, Visa or J.P. Morgan Gen Z fintech reports were retrieved.

## 8. Synthesis matrix (Inference, built from the evidence above)

| Problem | Teens complain? | Parents pay? | Programmable money materially better? | Strongest segment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Need a parent for every card/P2P/payout | Yes (structural) | Partially (Greenlight etc. are parent-owned) | Partial: a guardian-co-signed wallet; KYC still needed | 13–17 |
| Splitting / being paid back by friends | Yes (73%/76% stats, ages 16–26) | No | Moderate: escrow pots, auto-settle; but Venmo already exists | 16–19, college |
| Overspending / in-game spending visibility | Low | Yes ($6–20/mo tiers) | Moderate: rule-based allowances, merchant allowlists | 13–15, gamers |
| Scams / sextortion payouts | Yes (as victims) | Yes (safety tiers) | Weak to negative: irreversible transfers raise the risk unless allowlisted | 14–17 boys (sextortion) |
| Getting paid as an under-18 creator or freelancer | Yes | Maybe | Plausible: borderless receipts to a guardian-linked wallet; needs platform adoption | 15–17 creators, Roblox devs |
| Club / team / booster treasury | Adults & treasurers | Yes (avoiding fraud) | **Strong**: transparent ledger, multisig, purpose-locked funds | Club treasurers, parents |
| Gifts from relatives abroad | Likely | Likely | **Strong**: cheap, instant, borderless; off-ramp and KYC issues | Diaspora families, India/LatAm |
| Domestic everyday payments in India | Low (UPI Circle solves it) | Low (free) | **No**: UPI is free and instant | — |

By age (inference):
- **13–15:** parent-controlled, gaming-heavy, low earning.
- **16–17:** jobs, gigs and creator income start, and they hit the 18+ payout wall.
- **18–19:** legally adult, crypto-curious (the Gen Z adult stats apply), with splitting and rent pains.
