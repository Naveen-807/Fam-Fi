# Competitive Landscape: Teen/Youth Finance and Youth-Crypto Products (as of Oct 2026)

Research note: about 15 search/fetch calls. Where a claim comes only from my background knowledge and was NOT confirmed in this research pass, it is marked **[UNVERIFIED]** and kept out of the Cited Findings lists. Pricing pages conflict, so figures are given with their source.

## Q1. Competitor war-room table: who solves what, pricing, controls, what happens at 18, blockchain use

### Takeaway
Incumbents (Greenlight, GoHenry/Acorns, Revolut <18, Venmo/Cash App teen, Apple Cash Family, Google Wallet balance, India's UPI Circle/Paytm Pocket Money/FamApp/Junio) all use the same model. A parent funds a sub-account or prepaid card that they control, and the product is a spending tool with chores bolted on. Only Binance Junior and Cash App (bitcoin) touch crypto, and both keep it inside a closed, custodial system. Most do not let a teen send funds to the open chain, so nothing portable carries past 18.

### Inferences
- **Who pays:** Western incumbents charge parents a subscription (Greenlight, GoHenry). Big platforms (Venmo, Cash App, Apple, Google, Revolut) give the teen product away free to acquire users and lock in the family. In India it is free UPI or prepaid. A new entrant cannot win on price against free big tech, so it must win on capabilities they structurally don't offer.
- **Moats:** For Greenlight and GoHenry, it is brand plus the parent-subscription habit. For Apple, Google, Venmo and Cash App, it is distribution and existing parent accounts. For UPI apps, it is the NPCI rails. None of them has a data or reputation moat that belongs to the teen.
- **Shared fragility:** Copper and Step both relied on BaaS sponsor banks (Synapse, Evolve). Copper shows that a teen fintech's custody layer is a single point of failure. Self-custodial or smart-contract custody with transparent reserves is a direct answer to the "my kid's money vanished in 24 hours" story.

## Q2. Which products let teens EARN, receive from non-parents, carry history past 18, and share group money?

### Takeaway
"Earning" in incumbents means chores the parent pays for. Real earning from outside the family exists only in Venmo direct deposit, Cash App and Apple Cash P2P, and Roblox DevEx, a siloed gaming economy. Cross-border gifts (for example a grandparent abroad) are not supported by any product found. Only Step makes a credit claim past 18, and no product offers group or club treasuries for teens.

### Cited Findings
- Chore and allowance "earning" is parent-funded: Greenlight Core chores and allowance — [CNBC Select](https://www.cnbc.com/select/greenlight-review/); Junio tasks and allowance — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/personal-finance/banking/story/what-is-upi-circle-how-the-new-feature-is-making-digital-payments-safer-for-families-teenagers-534941-2026-06-05)
- External earnings: Venmo Teen direct deposit for wages from after-school jobs — [PayPal](https://newsroom.paypal-corp.com/2023-05-22-Introducing-the-Venmo-Teen-Account). Roblox creators can cash out Earned Robux via DevEx — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/roblox-creators-will-now-earn-85-more-from-developer-exchange)
- Receiving from non-parents is explicitly blocked or restricted at Google Wallet (parent money only) — [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/google-wallet-balance-for-under-18s-blocks-all-online-and-in-app-payments/) and at Binance Junior (no transfers to non-parental adults) — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/350792/binance-new-crypto-savings-account-kids). It is allowed but limited at Apple Cash Family (send and receive in Messages, with parent-restricted recipients) — [Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-il/105010) and at Cash App (P2P receive limits) — [Gerald](https://joingerald.com/learn/banking--payments/how-cash-app-teen-accounts-work-families)
- Family/friends contributions: Acorns Early lets family and friends invest in a child's future, which is the closest thing to grandparent gifting — [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230403005378/en/Acorns-Acquires-GoHenry-Delivering-Financial-Wellness-for-Lifes-Many-Stages)
- Past 18: Step markets its card as helping teens "build positive credit" — [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20200930005318/en/Step-Launches-No-Fee-Banking-App-Built-for-Teens). Revolut gives 16-17s a restricted account, implying a path to an adult account — [Revolut T&Cs](https://revolut.com/en-US/legal/under18-cfsb/). Greenlight anecdotally kept billing after a child turned 18, which suggests there is no designed graduation — [Gerald](https://joingerald.com/learn/financial-wellness/greenlight-reviews)
- UPI Circle and Paytm Pocket Money are spend-only delegations from the parent's bank account. The teen holds no balance or history of their own — [The Paypers](https://thepaypers.com/payments/news/paytm-launches-pocket-money-upi-feature-for-teenagers)

### Inferences
- **Group/shared money:** No product found offers a teen-run club treasury, multi-signer approval, or bill splitting beyond basic P2P. That is a clear gap, and multisig or Safe-style shared accounts handle it natively. Two caveats: this conclusion is based on absence in the sources, and P2P splitting exists in Venmo and Cash App.
- **Cross-border family money:** No found product lets a grandparent in another country send to a teen's supervised account cheaply. In India, UPI Circle is domestic only, and US teen products need US accounts. Stablecoin transfers make this cheap and nearly instant, and that is something blockchain can do that incumbents cannot.
- **Reputation:** The only teen "history" that exists stays inside each company's database and is lost when the teen leaves or the company dies (Copper). A portable, teen-owned on-chain record of chores done, savings streaks, gigs completed and treasury roles held is unclaimed. Examples are soulbound or attestation-based records such as EAS.

### Gaps
- No source confirmed whether any teen account's transaction history feeds a credit bureau, beyond Step's marketing claim. Whether Step reports to bureaus for under-18s is unverified.
- Youth creator payout platforms other than Roblox (YouTube, Twitch, Fortnite UEFN) were not researched. **[UNVERIFIED]** These generally require an adult or guardian AdSense or payout account.

## Q3. How crowded is "programmable allowance on-chain" / "teen stablecoin wallet" among hackathon and startup projects?

### Takeaway
The basic idea is fairly crowded at hackathon level: at least five recent projects, one on Monad testnet, built parent-funded smart-account allowances with delegation, time locks or task bounties. None has become a live funded product found in this research. The differentiation has to go beyond "allowance + spending caps".

### Cited Findings
- Playroom (ETHGlobal Taipei): cBTC savings wallet for kids. Parents deposit task rewards and allowance into a contract, kids mark tasks done, and Self Protocol age proof (18+) unlocks full withdrawal. Won Citrea and Curvegrid prizes — [ETHGlobal showcase](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/playroom-wykmm)
- Piggy Wallet (ETHGlobal Brussels finalist): Safe smart wallet via permissionless.js with a Pimlico paymaster. Features time-locked savings, USDC auto-swaps, goals, task bounties, an AI coach and parental controls — [ETHGlobal showcase](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/piggy-wallet-szyeh)
- TeenFi Allowance POC (HackQuest, ERC-7715 hackathon): parents set allowances and spending caps, and teens spend within approved categories using MetaMask ERC-7715 delegations on Sepolia — [HackQuest](https://www.hackquest.io/projects/ERC7715-Hackathon-TeenFi-Allowance-POC)
- Pocket Money (HackQuest): built on **Monad testnet**, where kids withdraw on their own using MetaMask Smart Account delegations — [HackQuest](https://hackquest.io/projects/Pocket-Money)
- MinorMint (lablab.ai, Apr 2026): AI-guided teen wallet where a Gemini "AI Guardian" replaces rigid parental controls — [lablab.ai](https://lablab.ai/submissions/wbbwhpyu2t6cn8sthba641ux)
- Other related ETHGlobal entries showed up in search but were not examined (minihackers, AllowFlow) — [ETHGlobal minihackers](https://ethglobal.com/showcase/minihackers-i9w55); [AllowFlow docs](https://broken-scooter-d9e.notion.site/AllowFlow-Documentation-8f4fb0627b72401abcced9c9a12e37ec)
- On the incumbent side, Binance Junior already offers custodial "crypto savings for kids" with yield — [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/350792/binance-new-crypto-savings-account-kids)

### Inferences
- Judges at Monad or other EVM hackathons have likely seen "parent sets allowance with a delegation/session key" several times. A Monad project with exactly that premise already exists, which is a direct collision risk for a Monad Metropolis entry.
- Under-built angles across these projects:
  - Earning from non-parents with guardian co-sign: gigs, fans, cross-border family.
  - Group treasuries run by teens.
  - A portable reputation or credential layer that persists at 18.
  - A designed "graduation" handover of keys or policy at 18. Playroom touches this with age-proof unlock but nothing more.
  - Real fiat on/off-ramps, such as UPI or cards, for parents who aren't crypto-native.

### Gaps
- Devpost, Solana and Base hackathon galleries were not searched directly within the budget, so the crowding count is a lower bound.
- No funded startup selling an on-chain teen stablecoin wallet was found. Absence is not proof. **[UNVERIFIED]** I am not aware of any live, funded "Safe-based family wallet" consumer product. Kidsrules and "bitcoin allowance" apps were not verified.

## Q4. Funding/shutdown lessons and the white space blockchain can fill

### Takeaway
The category's failures were about custody and plumbing (Copper/Synapse) and unit economics (subscription fatigue, consolidation via Acorns-GoHenry), not demand. Big tech (Apple, Google, PayPal, Block) and India's UPI Circle have made basic supervised spending a free commodity. The white space is what their closed, custodial, domestic, parent-funded design cannot do: open-world earning, cross-border inflows, teen-run group money, transparent self-custody that can't be frozen by a middleware collapse, and a teen-owned history that survives 18 and the provider.

### Cited Findings
- Copper's 24-hour shutdown and fund lockout came from a BaaS middleware failure. Synapse froze about $160M across fintechs, and Juno, Yotta and Mainvest were also hit — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/05/21/teen-fintech-copper-had-to-emergency-discontinue-its-banking-debit-products); [Banking Dive](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/fintech-copper-burned-by-synapse-collapse/717407/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/08/22/synapses-collapse-has-frozen-nearly-160m-from-fintech-users-heres-how-it-happened)
- Consolidation: GoHenry was sold into Acorns in an all-stock deal with undisclosed terms after being valued at $250-500M in 2022 — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2522716)
- Big tech and platforms entered free: Venmo Teen (2023), Google Wallet under-18 balance (2026), Apple Cash Family, and Paytm and Google Pay via UPI Circle (2026) — [PayPal](https://newsroom.paypal-corp.com/2023-05-22-Introducing-the-Venmo-Teen-Account); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=3150666); [Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-il/105010); [The Paypers](https://thepaypers.com/payments/news/paytm-launches-pocket-money-upi-feature-for-teenagers)
- Step diversified beyond teen banking, into a mobile phone plan and a Gen Z positioning — [Step via search; CNBC Top Fintech 2025 listing](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220412005546/en/Step-Announces-Plans-for-a-New-Crypto-Stock-Investment-Product) (specific Step Mobile press release URL not retrieved; date unverified)
- Youth earning is large but siloed: Roblox paid creators more than $1.5B in 2025 — [Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/roblox-raises-creator-payouts-will-margins-face-pressure-2026)

### Inferences (white space statement)
**No product researched lets a teen:**
1. Earn from the open world, such as clients, fans and gig bounties, into a supervised account with guardian co-approval rather than parental ownership.
2. Receive money cheaply from family abroad. The grandparent-in-Dubai to teen-in-Chennai flow is unserved: UPI Circle is domestic and spend-only, and US products need US banking.
3. Run a shared group treasury with peers (club, team, fest fund) with multi-party approval and a transparent ledger.
4. Own a portable record of financial behaviour that survives turning 18 and survives the provider dying. Copper's users lost their history and briefly their money.
5. Graduate smoothly at 18, with control handed over by rule rather than by closing one account and opening another.

**What blockchain does that incumbents structurally can't:**
- Programmable guardian policies through smart accounts and delegations (ERC-4337/7715, Safe modules), enforced by code rather than one company's backend.
- Stablecoin rails for borderless, near-instant inflows.
- Multisig or club treasuries with a public audit trail.
- Self-custody, which removes the Synapse-style middleman failure.
- Portable attestations or soulbound reputation that a teen carries into adulthood.
- Automatic policy change at 18, for example via a zk age proof as in Playroom.

**Risks:**
- Regulatory: minors and crypto, KYC, India's RBI stance on crypto and PPI.
- Parental fear of crypto.
- Big tech could copy features, though not the open, cross-border, portable ones without abandoning their closed models.
- A crowded hackathon field around plain "allowance".

### Gaps
- Regulatory constraints for minors holding stablecoins in India, the US, the UK and the EU (including MiCA) were not researched here.
- No quantified market sizing (number of teen accounts per product) beyond GoHenry+Acorns at about 6M subscribers in 2023 and Step at 1M+ users in 2021.
